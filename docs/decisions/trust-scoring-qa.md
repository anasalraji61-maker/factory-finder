# trust scoring QA (2026-10-15)

QA of `trust-v2` with 25 synthetic profiles (`services/trust/tests/fixtures/trust_archetypes.json`, runner `tests/test_archetypes.py`): a established factory x4, b glossy new trader x3, c too-good-to-be-true price x3, d good supplier with few reports x3, e review farm x3, f expired certificates/documents x3, g mixed signals x3, h report bombing x3. Checks: bands/score/confidence/flags per profile, bilingual explanations, no credit for verification that did not happen, confidence monotonicity (removing or adding a signal never lowers/raises it the wrong way), gaming resistance.

Defects found and fixed in place (no contract change; all additive/internal, `model_version` stays `trust-v2`):
- 2026-10-15 Confidence was not monotonic: depth was the MEAN of reviews/transactions/deals depth, so adding a shallow signal lowered confidence. Now depth = MAX of depth parts (reviews, transactions, deals, years in business), 0 when none; confidence = coverage x (0.6 + 0.4 x depth). A glossy brand-new profile with many cheap claims now lands ~50 instead of ~70.
- 2026-10-15 A missing critical check (`sanctions_hit`, `dispute_rate`, `price_vs_market_ratio`, `verified`) could still give confidence >= 80 next to a green band. Confidence is now capped at 79 when any is missing (`CRITICAL_SIGNALS`).
- 2026-10-15 "Too good to be true" prices between 0.4x and 0.6x market could still be green. New cap `price_anomaly_cap` (green_min - 1 = 74) for ratio < `price_anomaly_low`; < 0.4x keeps the existing cap 59.
- 2026-10-15 Review farming: hundreds of near-perfect marketplace reviews on a tiny transaction history earned full rating/review credit. New check `review_pattern_suspicious` (>= 30 reviews, average >= 4.9, reviews > 2x transactions_count): rating and review-count factors get no credit, flag + bilingual info line. Needs `transactions_count`; without it nothing is flagged (no data, no accusation). Config: `review_burst_*`. Not applied to a flawless 5.0 alone (organic small shops exist; existing shrinkage test expects it).
- 2026-10-15 `ReportStore.accepted_count` counted accepted reports, so one account (or a moderator accepting five reports of one sockpuppet) multiplied the penalty. Now counts DISTINCT reporters.

Verified as already sound: pending reports (also from low-trust new accounts) never change the score; confirmed-report penalty is bounded (weight 8); duplicate documents count once per type; duplicate certificate spellings (ISO9001 / iso 9001 / ISO-9001) count once; expired certificates/documents earn nothing and raise `*_expired` flags; verification level is `none` without approved documents and `audited` needs an audit; every factor and gap has non-empty English and Arabic text.

Known limits (not changed): the fresh-review flag cannot see review timestamps (no burst-window signal exists in the contract; a request for `reviews_last_30d` would need an additive contract field, not filed); a flagged profile that later gains `transactions_count` shows the flag and lower score, but confidence does not drop (kept monotonic on purpose); `dispute_rate=0` on a handful of transactions still earns up to +4; junk certificate names are bounded (+3 max over two real ones) but not validated.
